About

I'm a first-year at Dartmouth College studying Computer Science. My research interests sit at the intersection of natural language processing, cognitive science, and the way language shapes — and is shaped by — the societies that speak it. There's something endlessly fascinating to me about the fact that a word can carry centuries of cultural memory inside it.

I grew up moving between China and Japan, which means I've spent my whole life living at the seam between cultures. That instilled an obsession with translation — not just between languages, but between ways of thinking. Now I find myself drawn to questions like: what gets lost when a dialect disappears? What does a neural network actually "learn" about meaning? How does social media accelerate or flatten cultural drift?

Outside research, I spend time on fashion and aesthetics, competitive puzzle math, gaming, writing, and analyzing how trends propagate on social platforms. I have a cat named Natto. I eat too many oranges.
